Read the workflow as text
  1. Input: Sample SAMN36633012 includes its title, host (Homo sapiens), host disease (Staphylococcus aureus bacteremia), and isolation source (blood from patients of a rural regional hospital).
  2. Proposer: Extract fields and select ontologies: UBERON for blood, MONDO for disease terms, and ENVO for hospital.
  3. Ontology RAG with FAISS: Retrieve the top 10 similar ontology terms. The figure shows blood (UBERON:0000178, confidence 1.0), staphylococcal infection (MONDO:0005545, 0.98), bacteremia (MONDO:0000108, 0.93), and hospital (ENVO:0002173, 0.82).
  4. Resolver: Select the best candidate terms and assign them to fields.
  5. Output: The sample's isolation_source contains UBERON:0000178 and ENVO:0002173; host_disease contains MONDO:0005545 and MONDO:0000108.

The Proposer and Resolver are shown within the LLM reasoning steps. This is an illustrated example, not live processing status.
